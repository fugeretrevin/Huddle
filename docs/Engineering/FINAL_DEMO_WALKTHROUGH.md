# Demo walkthrough

For the build of 2026-11-12 (redesigned layout). Standalone: no PC or key is needed; the plaque reads SCRIPTED DEMO and every card, paper and receipt repeats it. Verified in Editor Play Mode; not yet walked through on the headset.

Sit down and put the headset on. The office appears on the surface in front of you, agents facing you. About 90 seconds for one run.

| Step | You do | You see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look | Researcher, Developer and Tester at their desks, each with a nameplate reading Idle. Plaque: SCRIPTED DEMO. One line: "Press Start on the task dock." |
| 2 | Press **Start** on the dock, front left | Dock: "In progress: the agents are on it". The Researcher's plate reads "Reading the brief"; the Tester runs baseline tests |
| 3 | Wait about 20 s | The Researcher walks to the front holding a small paper marked DECISION. Dock: "Needs your decision on the paper" |
| 4 | Take the paper by its edge or the TAKE tab | It unfolds to full size: the question, a short summary, the recommendation, and the line "Template recommendation. Not live AI research." |
| 5 | Optional: poke **Why** or **Details** | A second sheet opens below with the reasoning, risk and impact |
| 6 | Press **Approve**, or press the green stamp on the stamp area | "Sending...", then "Approved" on the sheet; the paper glides to the out-tray |
| 7 | Watch | The Developer writes code, hands it to the Tester, the Tester runs the tests |
| 8 | Read the receipt, front right | "Delivered", the summary, tests passed and files changed, SCRIPTED DEMO |
| 9 | Optional: poke **Filed 1** on the out-tray | The archive readout shows what you decided and what came of it |
| 10 | Press **Run again** on the receipt | The run restarts |

Variations:
- **Reject** at step 6: nothing is built; the receipt reads "Stopped. You rejected the proposal."
- **Changes** at step 6: the Researcher comes back with a revised proposal (up to two revisions).
- **Board** on the dock opens the full task board at your left: grab a card to see the assignment pads, select one for Pause and Priority, New adds a task. Board again closes it.
- **Focus** at front right toggles focus mode (unchanged by the redesign; only the key moved).

With the desktop server running and reachable, the plaque reads LIVE and the same steps drive the real pipeline in the sandbox; the provenance line on the paper still says when a recommendation came from a template.
